### Erratum found and fixed during verification
The first restore drill of the pre-Stage-2 backup **failed (0/7 tables)**. The dump was fine (PGDMP header, full
18-table TOC); the drill script had a race: `pg_isready` over the unix socket succeeds against the Docker
entrypoint's *temporary init server*, so `pg_restore` could run just before the real restart. It passed at 06:33
by timing luck. Fixed (`-h 127.0.0.1` TCP readiness) in `providers/vps/backup/restore-test-aion-runtime.sh` and
on the box. Earlier "6/6" results were real (counts matched production), but were not race-proof until now.

## Remaining operational limitations (honest list)
- **Limits are sized from idle usage, not load.** Runtime/copilot/Postgres sit at 22–24 MiB against 512 MiB/1 GiB
  caps. No load test has run, so the caps are safety backstops, not tuned values; revisit with real traffic.
- **The box now runs the PR #13 compose, not `main`'s.** If someone `scp`s `providers/vps/docker-compose.yml` from
  `main` before #13 merges, the limits/rotation/grace period silently revert on the next recreate. Merge #13.
- **`postgres:16-alpine` is a floating tag.** The recreate reused the local image (ID unchanged), but a future
  pull + recreate could move the minor version. Recommend digest-pinning (`postgres@sha256:cf78e766…`).
- **Monitor is a 60 s poll.** The 22 s intake pause fell between polls and was invisible to it; any outage shorter
  than a poll interval is too. It detects sustained failures, not blips.
- **Log rotation applies to new containers only**; pre-rollout container logs were preserved by copy before the
  recreate and are on the box only (`/root/aion-rollout-20260920/`, 0600), not in any backup.
- Rollback artefacts are on this host only: the compose snapshot (also under `/opt/aion`) and
  `docker-compose.stage1-only.yml`.
